Transparent Methodology & Assumptions

  • 1 Meal = 400 g (0.4 kg) of balanced cooked food.
  • Greenhouse Gas Factor = 2.5 kg CO2e prevented per 1 kg of food waste diverted from landfills (based on FAO/UNEP food waste emission benchmarks).
  • Value Saved = ₹65 average meal equivalent value.
  • Meals = kg rescued ÷ 0.4
  • CO2e prevented = kg rescued × 2.5
  • Value saved = meals × ₹65
  • Average rescue turnaround = mean of (deliveredAt − postedAt) in minutes across Delivered batches.
